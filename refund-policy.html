Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Every product is handmade to order. Once payment is made, the order cannot be cancelled, returned, replaced or refunded.

Why we have this policy

We do not keep ready stock. We start making your product after your order and payment are confirmed, and the materials and time are used for you alone. For this reason, we cannot take an order back once it is paid.

What this means for you

  • No cancellation after payment is made.
  • No refund after payment is made.
  • No return or replacement, including for change of mind, colour or look preferences, or a wrong address supplied by the customer.
  • Please check your items, quantity, address and phone number carefully before you pay.

Handmade variations

Each piece is made by hand, so small differences in shape, colour and finish from the photos on our site are natural and are not defects.

If we cannot make your order

If we are unable to make or dispatch your order for any reason on our side, we will tell you on WhatsApp and refund the amount you paid in full.

Questions before you order

Ask us anything on WhatsApp before you pay. See our Contact page, Shipping policy and Terms.
