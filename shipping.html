Shipping & Delivery

Made to order. Usually ready and dispatched within 2–3 days of payment confirmation. We deliver all over India.

Making your order

Our products are handmade after you order. We start once your payment is confirmed on WhatsApp. This usually takes 2–3 days. Larger quantities or custom requests may take longer, and we will tell you before you pay.

Delivery

  • We ship all over India.
  • Delivery charges are currently included in the product price. You pay no extra amount at checkout.
  • If we introduce separate delivery charges in future, the amount will be shown or confirmed with you before you pay.
  • Delivery time depends on your location and the courier. We will share tracking or delivery updates on WhatsApp where available.

Your address

Please give a complete address with pincode and a working mobile number. We are not responsible for delays or failed delivery caused by an incorrect or incomplete address.
